# DIZRUPT ENGINEERING UNIVERSITY

Welcome to **DizruptOS Engineering University**.

This curriculum is not standard documentation. It is a rigorous, reverse-engineered educational framework designed to take a senior engineer and elevate them to **Founder-Level Mastery** of the DizruptOS platform. 

By the end of this curriculum, you will not just know *how* to use the repository, you will know exactly *why* every decision was made, the math behind it, and how to rebuild it from scratch.
